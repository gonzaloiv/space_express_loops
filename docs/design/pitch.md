## Goal

What the submission says and shows: the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026, due **Friday
2026-11-13**. The deliverables work (capture, video, form, store image) starts **Wednesday
2026-11-11**; until then the time goes to the game itself.

## The entry

- **Track:** Gaming. **Division:** New Experience — the game was conceived and built inside the
  competition window.
- **The angle:** you bend spacetime with your bare hands, and the universe sits on your desk. The
  competition's own line is "the future of VR is in your hands, literally"; the pinch inside the cube
  is that line. Lead with the hands, not with the transport-company frame.
- **The hero moment — the stretch:** a hand pinches inside the cube in passthrough, the lattice and
  planets stretch, the predicted path swings, release, the ship arrives. It opens the video and is the
  store image. It also sets the game priorities before capture: the arrival payoff and the cube's
  sound must land first.

## Tagline candidates (≤140 characters)

1. Bend spacetime with your bare hands. A tiny universe on your desk, one cube to reshape it, and every delivery has to arrive.
2. Shrink space, not the ship. A seated MR puzzle where your hands warp the universe on your table.
3. A pocket universe in your room. Pinch the cube, stretch spacetime, get the cargo home.

## The video (under 3 minutes, best material first)

1. **0:00–0:10** — the stretch, then the title.
2. **0:10–0:30** — the universe on your desk; Jim hands over the first job.
3. **0:30–1:30** — three jobs that show the lessons building up: scale, clear, move the cube.
4. **1:30–2:00** — the entropy budget: a fail, a retry, the stars.
5. **2:00–2:30** — the map fills; the same cube picks the next job.
6. **2:30–2:50** — what comes next: chapters and the store release ([chapters](chapters.md)).

## The judges' tests

How the pitch answers the design guidelines:

- **Hands-first:** the whole run is pinch and grab; no controller at any point.
- **Airplane seat:** the bubble and the cube sit at seated arm's reach; nothing needs standing or
  turning around.
- **One bus stop:** a job is ~1 minute; seven jobs fill ~10 minutes; the first job opens straight
  from launch.

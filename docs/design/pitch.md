## Goal

What the submission says and shows: the Meta VR Start Developer Competition 2026. We freeze on
**Friday 2026-11-13**; the official window runs to **2026-11-18, 12:00 PM PT**, which is the margin.
The deliverables work (capture, video, form) starts **Wednesday 2026-11-11**; until then the time
goes to the game itself.

## The entry

- **Track:** Gaming. **Division:** New Experience — the game was conceived and built inside the
  competition window on a fresh project (only two models carried over from a scaffold).
- **The angle:** you bend spacetime with your bare hands, and the universe sits on your desk. The
  competition's own line is "the future of VR is in your hands, literally"; the two-hand pinch inside
  the cube is that line. Lead with the hands, not with the transport-company frame.
- **The hero moment — the stretch:** both hands pinch inside the cube in passthrough, the lattice and
  planets stretch, the predicted path swings, release, the ship arrives. It opens the video and is the
  store image. It also sets the game priorities before capture: the arrival payoff and the cube's
  sound must land first.

## Tagline candidates (≤140 characters)

1. Bend spacetime with your bare hands. A tiny universe on your desk, one cube to reshape it, and every delivery has to arrive.
2. Shrink space, not the ship. A seated MR puzzle where your hands warp the universe on your table.
3. A pocket universe in your room. Two hands on the cube, stretch spacetime, get the cargo home.

## The video (under 3 minutes, best material first)

Target ~2:30. Judges may stop early, so the first 20 seconds have to sell the game alone. Burn in
captions: many judges watch muted.

| Time | Shot | On-screen line |

**Capture notes.** Record each beat separately with several takes on the frozen build, using Meta's
on-device capture (enable the 16:9 crop if offered). Keep the head still, move the hands slowly and
keep the cube centred in frame. Light the room well (passthrough gets noisy in the dark) and clear
the desk. Turn off notifications. Keep the game's own audio (music, Jim, cube sounds) and add music
only where it ducks under Jim.

**Screenshots** (reuse the frames from the video): the stretch, a ship arriving, the fleet, the lit
map, plus the store cover shot.

## The judges' tests

How the pitch answers the design guidelines:

- **Hands-first:** the whole run is pinch and grab; no controller at any point.
- **Airplane seat:** the bubble and the cube sit at seated arm's reach; nothing needs standing or
  turning around.
- **One bus stop:** a job is ~1 minute; eleven jobs fill ~11–15 minutes; the first job opens straight
  from launch.

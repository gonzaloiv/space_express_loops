# Progression — "Our Space"

**Status:** design. How the game advances across jobs. The
[campaign menu](../../plans/backlog/gameplay/space-shaping-future.md) is the build sketch; this
is the design.

**Related:** [project direction](project-direction.md) · [transport company](transport-company.md) · [lore](lore.md) · [lens plan](../plans/in-progress/space-shaping-lens.md)

## 1. The question

Two things: **what advances**, and **what gates**.

## 2. Three layers

1. **The spine (what gates).** Solve job N → job N+1 opens. A linear *order* paces the run; you
   cannot wander into job 9 at minute one.
2. **The network (what you build).** The map *is* the company: solved cubes lit, current
   highlighted, locked dark. Every solve **grows the network** — the transport-company fantasy,
   made visible.
3. **The score (what you chase).** Per-job **stars** (the **entropy left** / eco rating, bucketed
   1–3) plus a running total. That is *mastery*, separate from *breadth*.

So progression has **two axes** — breadth (light every cube) and depth (3-star each). Different
players chase different ones.

## 3. The map — numbered cubes in a big universe

- A **big universe** (the bounded sector field,
  [sector generation](../plans/done/universe-sector-generation.md)) holds the cubes, one per job.
- Each cube carries a **number** — the number *is* the order. The graph is **implied**, not drawn:
  no route lines to build or read.
- The layout gives the spatial "big universe" feel; the numbers give the sequence.

## 4. The learning path — the cube grows

The gestures are taught in order, so the player never faces two at once:

1. **Jobs 1–2 — the fixed cube.** The cube comes at a **fixed size** (a fixed region); the only
   move is the **grab-inside scale** (the space + the content). One lever, one planet, a generous
   window.
2. **Job 3 — multiple scales.** The player places and scales **several zones**; the soft brush
   blends them, so "where do I scale" enters. The cube can also **resize** (the region).
3. **Job 4+ — rotate.** The spin gesture unlocks (frame dragging — a spinning zone swirls the
   path), adding *direction* to the scale.

So the order is **scale → multiple scales → rotate**; each lands once the previous is understood.

## 5. The company is lore only

No economy, no ships, no unlocks for now. The fiction wraps the map; progression is the **lit
cubes + stars**, nothing that ticks live. Reputation / ships / systems are the wrapper, once the
core loop is fun.

## 6. The 10-minute shape

About **8–10 cubes**, ~1 minute each. The curve rises by **planet count** and **window
tightness**; the map visibly fills as you go — that filling *is* the progression feel.

### Variation axes (enough for ~10 distinct jobs)

The verb is always the **scale**, so variety comes from the field and the brief, not new gestures:

1. **Planet count** — 1 → 4+ sources.
2. **Window tightness** — generous `[Tmin, Tmax]` → a razor window.
3. **Entropy budget** — loose → tight (grade pressure).
4. **Launch** — the failed launch's direction/speed changes per job.
5. **Well depth** — shallow (mild dilation) → deep (the clock crawls).
6. **Layout** — single well vs multi-well; where the shortcut zone sits vs the natural path.
7. **Scramble** — how far the authored start sits from the solved arrangement (the difficulty dial).

Jobs 1–2 teach the scale on one planet; job 3 adds multiple scales and planet count; job 4 adds
rotate. Ten jobs is the stretch goal — prove 3–5 first (lens L2), then extend only if the axes
hold.

## 7. Open

- **Star-gating?** No, in the demo — never block a run on a score. Stars feed a rating, or unlock
  *cosmetics / bonus* later.
- **Map interaction:** pick a cube directly, or only "next"? (Sequential order suggests "next".)
- **The numbers:** reset per act, or run 1 → N?

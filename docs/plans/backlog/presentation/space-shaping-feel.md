# Space Shaping — Feel & Presentation Polish (backlog)

**Status:** later · presentation · updated 2026-10-26 — collected, design only, **saved for later**. These are the *feel* beats that
make the core loop land, after the lens gate passes and
the puzzle content exists — a working game first. None of these gate the mechanic; they are the
polish that turns "works" into "wow". Not current work: the focus is **interesting puzzles**.

**Related:** [vision](../../../design/vision.md) · [lore](../../../design/world/lore.md)

## 1. The flight (the payoff beat)

- The ship **flies on every modification** — the flight *is* the reveal (the prediction line is gone,
  [travel plan](../../in-progress/space-shaping-travel.md)). The payoff is the launch-and-land beat: a
  run that lands reads as a win; a miss reads as a failure.
- A **haptic "stretch"** on the cube scale, and a rising **pitch** as entropy drains — the cube is
  the product; it should *feel* like it is pulling spacetime.

## 2. Collapse feedback

- As entropy drains, the universe visibly **wobbles / deforms** (the collapse). Start as pure
  feedback; optionally, later, let the deformation push the path (a *mechanical* collapse).

## 3. The window as a diegetic gauge (+ comedy)

- The `[Tmin, Tmax]` window shown as a **green band** on the ship's clock, not two numbers.
- Lean into the `Tmin` oddity as the *customer*: "arrive between 4:02 and 4:03 — not a minute
  early." The absurd-precision deadline is a joke, not an arbitrary rule.

## 4. Cargo / customer personality

- Each job gets a **reason** (the ice cube, the birthday card, the duck). One line of flavour per
  level, drawn from [lore](../../../design/world/lore.md)'s deadpan tone.

## 5. Entropy as a diegetic object

- Tie the entropy bar to the collapse so the *world* is the meter, not a HUD number: the
  neighbourhood dims/warps as the bar drains.

## 6. HUD / audio

- The HUD sits **over the level** (world-anchored) — it works today; it could also ride the TTS panel
  screen ([voice panel](tts-voice-panel-personality.md)).
- **Music exists**; the **new SFX** (travel, scale, grade, collapse) are the audio work to add.
- The **collapse** is deferred: for now the entropy bar depletes and a simple "you lose" feedback
  plays ([resources](../../../design/systems/resources.md)).

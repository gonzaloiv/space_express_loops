# Space Shaping — Feel & Presentation Polish (backlog)

**Status:** backlog - design only, collected. These are the *feel* beats that make the core
loop land, **after** the [lens](../in-progress/space-shaping-lens.md) gate passes — a working
game first. None of these gate the mechanic; they are the polish that turns "works" into
"wow".

**Related:** [lens plan](../in-progress/space-shaping-lens.md) · [project direction](../../design/project-direction.md) · [lore](../../design/lore.md)

## 1. The travel moment (the payoff beat)

- The trajectory locks **green** when it is right; the ship lights up and **glides** on its own
  (no button). This departure is the emotional payoff of the loop.
- A **haptic "stretch"** on the cube scale, and a rising **pitch** as entropy drains — the cube is
  the product; it should *feel* like it is pulling spacetime.

## 2. Collapse feedback

- As entropy drains, the universe visibly **wobbles / deforms** (the collapse). Start as pure
  feedback; optionally, later, let the deformation push the path (a *mechanical* collapse).

## 3. The window as a diegetic gauge (+ comedy)

- The `[Tmin, Tmax]` window shown as a **green band** on the ship's clock, not two numbers.
- Lean into the `Tmin` oddity as the *customer*: "arrive between 4:02 and 4:03 — not a minute
  early." The absurd-precision deadline is a joke, not an arbitrary rule.

## 4. Cargo / customer personality

- Each job gets a **reason** (the ice cube, the birthday card, the duck). One line of flavour per
  level, drawn from [lore](../../design/lore.md)'s deadpan tone.

## 5. Entropy as a diegetic object

- Tie the entropy bar to the collapse so the *world* is the meter, not a HUD number: the
  neighbourhood dims/warps as the bar drains.

## Order (after the gate)

1 → 2 → 3, with 4 and 5 alongside. The travel moment (1) is the demo beat; the collapse (2) is
the depth; the window gauge (3) makes the hard fail legible.

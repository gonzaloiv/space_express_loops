**Status:** next · flow · updated 2026-11-19 — 20 prebaked, hand-curated contracts, opened after the
story as one extra system on the map.

**Bakes:** off the editor — U1 (~40 candidates) ([AGENTS](../../../AGENTS.md#bakes-run-off-the-editor), `docs/tools/bake.ps1`).

## Goal

After the ending, the map shows one more system with 20 freelance contracts. Each is graded with 1–3
stars, has one client and cargo line, and no Jim intro. It is the story's epilogue: Jim's job board.

## Units

### U1 — bake ~40 candidates

- Contracts across the taught mechanics (Curve, Clear, fleets, multi-zone) at mixed difficulty,
  using the existing bakes and the background search.
- Exit: ~40 candidate assets in a freelance folder.

### U2 — play and keep 20

- Play each on the headset; keep 20 that are fun and fair, ordered easy → hard. Playing them replaces
  the automatic quality filter.
- Exit: 20 assets kept; the rest deleted.

### U3 — the chapter and the map system

- A freelance chapter asset: no intro, one client and cargo line per job, normal stars. It unlocks
  after the ending and shows as one extra system on the map (no tiers, no job board).
- Exit: the system appears only after the ending; its jobs open and save stars.

### U4 — lines

- 20 client and cargo lines in the release's voice.
- Exit: every contract has its card.

## Manual check (headset)

1. Before the ending, the freelance system is hidden.
2. After the ending, it appears and opens on the first contract.
3. A contract has no Jim intro; the card shows client and cargo.
4. Stars save and show on the map.
5. Time five contracts; the target is ~1.5 min each.

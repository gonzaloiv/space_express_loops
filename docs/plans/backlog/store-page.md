# Store page

**Status:** next · presentation · updated 2026-11-19 — art, text, age rating, privacy policy and the
demo option, ready before submission.

**Related:** [plans board](../README.md) · [release](../../design/release.md) · [pitch](../../design/pitch.md)

## Goal

Everything the submission needs on the store page is done and filled in the developer dashboard, so
the release build is the only thing left on submit day.

## Units

### U1 — the list and the review time

- List the paid app's store requirements from Meta's current docs: art sizes, text fields, the age
  rating questionnaire, the privacy policy, data use and the demo option.
- Find out how long review takes now for a new paid app. It decides the submission date
  ([release build](release-build.md)), so do this first.
- Exit: the checklist and the review time written in this plan.

### U2 — text

- Name, short and long description, from the [pitch](../../design/pitch.md) and the competition form.
  Sell 40–45 minutes of story plus freelance; do not promise the daily contract.
- Exit: the text is in the dashboard.

### U3 — art and capture

- Hero art, cover, screenshots and a trailer cut from on-device footage (reuse the competition video
  where it still matches).
- Exit: assets uploaded at the required sizes.

### U4 — rating, privacy and demo

- The age rating questionnaire, a hosted privacy policy page, the data use form, and the demo option
  (which jobs it includes).
- Exit: all filled; the dashboard shows nothing missing except the build.

## Manual check

1. The store page preview reads right in the headset store.
2. The screenshots match the release build (chapter 3 included).
3. The demo launches and stops where intended.
